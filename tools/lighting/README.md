# Synaxis reception lighting pilot

`synaxis_reception_bake.tscn` contains an isolated 5.30 × 4.18 m reception bay with a thick floor, ceiling and walls, a recessed fixture and three explicit probe positions. `reception_shell.glb` is original Blender geometry generated by `tools/asset_refinement/gen2_synaxis_luminaire.py --bake-shell`. Its SHA-256 is `f56b7215b8dea7efb169fb7acf373d2dd98a8e6b5ca5a8694bc905c452f64f31`. The incorporated marble photograph is listed in `assets/pbr/synaxis_architecture/SOURCE.json`.

The two GLB import presets use Static Lightmaps and 5 cm texel spacing. The bake environment excludes the sky. The emissive diffuser supplies indirect light during baking and the separate realtime spotlight owns direct illumination and dynamic shadows. Open the scene in the Godot 4.7.2 editor and bake `ReceptionGI` when the GPU is available. Inspect its output at the desk, staff probe positions and across the open approach before transplanting the bake to the production lobby.

`godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/lighting/validate_synaxis_reception.gd` checks the imported UV2 inputs and lighting ownership. The pilot stays editor-only. The production reception uses the same authored fixture with its established spotlight and can be compared with the single AreaLight3D through the explicit `--experimental-reception-area-light` user argument.
