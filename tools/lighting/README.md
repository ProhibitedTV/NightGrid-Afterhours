# Synaxis reception lighting pilot

`synaxis_reception_bake.tscn` contains an isolated 5.30 × 4.18 m reception bay with a thick floor, ceiling and walls, a recessed fixture and three explicit probe positions. `reception_shell.glb` is original Blender geometry generated by `tools/asset_refinement/gen2_synaxis_luminaire.py --bake-shell`. Its SHA-256 is `f56b7215b8dea7efb169fb7acf373d2dd98a8e6b5ca5a8694bc905c452f64f31`. The incorporated marble photograph is listed in `assets/pbr/synaxis_architecture/SOURCE.json`.

The two GLB import presets use Static Lightmaps and 5 cm texel spacing. The bake environment excludes the sky. The emissive diffuser supplies indirect light during baking and the separate realtime spotlight owns direct illumination and dynamic shadows. The checked-in EXR and lightmap data were baked in the Godot 4.7.2 Windows editor on the RTX 3060. The directional atlas is 512 × 256 across eight layers, with eleven mesh assignments. Open the scene and bake `ReceptionGI` again after geometry, UV2, lighting or probe changes.

`godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/lighting/validate_synaxis_reception.gd` checks the imported UV2 inputs, lightmap bindings and lighting ownership. The pilot is excluded from the Windows creator export. Its native comparison holds the camera and realtime spotlight fixed and samples diffuse bounce on three dynamic witnesses. Pass a new absolute evidence directory when running it from a Godot 4.7.2 source checkout:

```powershell
godot --path . --fullscreen --resolution 1920x1080 --rendering-method forward_plus --rendering-driver vulkan --script res://tools/lighting/capture_synaxis_reception_bake.gd -- <new-evidence-directory>
```

The production reception uses the same authored fixture with its established spotlight. The optional `--experimental-reception-area-light` user argument selects the AreaLight3D comparison. A production bake still needs a stable room chunk and animated-character inspection along the open approach.
