# Morrow OS — integration core

P opens the phone on foot, or use Smartphone in the pause menu. Nine apps expose existing live game state and route to physical destinations. No phone-side payouts, save schema changes, remote purchases, or automatic contract acceptance. Music and street cred reflect the currently merged PocketBeat / Blue Hour career. The older PR #315 retains its additional band, merchandise, work-offer and duty mechanics for separate review. This slice does not enable those features. Native visual and controller playtest pending.

## QuotaDrop offer selection
Before collecting an order, Decline / next offer cycles through the six authored offers without payment or progress. Carried orders cannot be declined; use the existing abandon/retry flow. The optional bounded `courier_offer_offset` save field preserves the chosen route, including mid-delivery; old saves default to zero. Duty toggles and additional musician progression are separate work.

## Technical consulting
Workboard lists two optional one-time diagnosis contracts at Crane & Gear and Grid Annex Four. Visit during open hours, examine the fictional evidence and select a repair. Wrong choices do not pay; wallet-capacity failure remains retryable. Completion uses the existing receipt ledger. No additional save schema or remote payout. The Paper City follow-up investigation is not included in this slice.

## The Paper City
After either technical consulting contract, a four-stage evidence investigation opens at Harbor Reading Room, Southworks Records and Dock Watch. Workboard routes to the current lead and the journal tracks progress. The final choice distinguishes documented inconsistencies from unsupported accusations. The existing ledger pays once; a bounded optional save stage is validated against prerequisites and completion receipt. Legacy saves remain valid.

## Off-duty mode
Go off duty before collecting cargo to hide the delivery beacon, radar/map pin, route action and pickup prompt. Resume the same offer from QuotaDrop without payment or progression changes. An active carried order must be delivered or abandoned first. The optional boolean save field defaults to on duty for old saves; carried orders cannot be restored off duty. The story journal directs the player to resume QuotaDrop instead of routing to an inactive delivery.

## Band recruitment
After buying and practicing the PocketBeat M3 three times and completing a legitimate Blue Hour opening set, invite Rhea Vale at Copper Cup's music desk and Milo Static at Dock Nine's staffed counter. Both choices cost nothing and award no cash or cred. The bounded roster lives in the existing progression ledger and the Music app reports collaborators. Older saves default to no recruits. Rehearsals, instrument-specific band performance, naming and merchandise remain later slices.

## Band rehearsal
The recruited trio rehearses at Copper Cup via its existing music desk. Two distinct 16-beat patterns, 13 required hits, 0.72-second beats, 0.20-second hit window. Successful claims at the actual reachable desk record one bounded rehearsal; retries, cancelled sets and duplicates do not count. No cash or cred. Optional roster-validated count survives saves; two rehearsals unlock the next booking slice (not yet implemented). The pads are existing sampler audio, not full band audio or animated NPC performance.
