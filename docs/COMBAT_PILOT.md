# Combat pilot / equipment UX
1. On foot outdoors, Q / D-pad Left cycles holstered, Vanta Pulse and Coilbreak Arc. Hold RMB / LT to aim over the shoulder; LMB / RT fires a single shot per press; Z / D-pad Up reloads. No fire without aim. Existing vehicle triggers, supplies, contacts, radio, menu actions and debug bindings remain intact.
2. Starter arcade loadout is available immediately for playtest; weapons have distinct fictional capacity, cooldown and impact values. Magazine and reserve are shown on a context-specific card, journal lists both; a centre-screen sight indicates aiming and confirms vehicle impacts.
3. Actual Godot raycasts respect solid collision from both view and player origin. Existing player-owned/street vehicles and police cruisers can be damaged down to their existing condition floor. Disabled police no longer chase or apply contact damage. Shots raise the existing police heat; no human NPC combat, pedestrian damage, shops, ballistics simulation or autonomous gun combat yet.
4. First-pass original sci-fi prop meshes, one-arm pose, cached synthetic sound on WorldSFX bus. Native silhouette, animation, camera aim, audio and actual frame pacing remain a visual/playtest gate.
5. Optional v1 `weapons` save data persists ammo and reserve; old saves get starter loadout, all restores are holstered. Reload/cooldown are transient. No actual weapon design or manufacturing geometry.
6. Test validates state machine, ammo conservation, real-world vehicle ray hit, heat, HUD and pause/drive gates. Follow up: equipment acquisition, ammo resupply, enemy NPC AI, authored higher fidelity character rigs and weapons, contextual radial selector, varied damage feedback and mission integration.
