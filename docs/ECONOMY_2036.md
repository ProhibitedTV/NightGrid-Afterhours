# Morrow Bay economy — 2036 baseline

Creator direction: nothing is free; USD and BTC are normal currencies. Use estimated 2026 US prices multiplied by (1.03)^10 = 1.343916379, rounded once at point of sale. 3% is a fictional planning assumption, not an economic forecast. The Federal Reserve's 2% long-run objective is a reference, not a guarantee: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260318a.htm . Use BLS CPI as a calibration source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.htm . Category scarcity premiums should be documented, not silently compounded.

Wardrobe prices are illustrative 2026 anchors: $35–45 everyday clothing, $75 branded, $300 premium, $950 luxury, $2,400 exclusive. The protagonist's existing Dispatch outfit is already owned, not a free acquisition. All additional clothing costs money. Re-equipping owned clothing is not a new purchase.

USD is the existing whole-dollar wallet. BTC uses integer satoshis (100 million per BTC), simulated fixed $250,000/BTC for now (400 sats/USD); this is an authored gameplay rate, not a prediction or live quote. Both wallets persist, with bounded validation; wardrobe accepts both currencies. Home exchange terminal trades $100/40,000 sats with a 2% fee. No real cryptocurrency integration, network or keys. Future price changes must not alter owned items or historical balances.

Next pass: review all vending, dining, transport, fuel, medical, repair, equipment, property and vehicle prices using modern analogues, tune earnings and avoid softlocks. Replace free home and starter car grants with earned/paid lease or purchase paths; maintain accessible recovery and a viable opening job at zero cash. Paid transit, utilities, maintenance, rent, clinic services and vehicle fuel must be balanced together, not added as disconnected punitive taxes. Expand BTC tender to other merchants and job payments; allow physical cash and electronic USD to share one bounded balance. Avoid live price fetching and arbitrary simulation overhead. Mandatory story progression should not depend on luxury clothing; optional social paths may.
