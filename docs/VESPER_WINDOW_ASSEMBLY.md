# Vesper Market window assembly pilot

Part of #19/#21. The two ordinary buildings on the Moon Noodles parcel
(-3,-3) no longer use individual flat front window rectangles and separate
sill boxes. Original UV-mapped contiguous frame rings, reveal faces, glazing,
mullions, transoms and sills are grouped into two or three shared-material
surfaces per building. Warm glazing is restrained emission, not new lights.
The window pane has depth relative to the frame; this is NOT a real opening
through the existing solid shell. Rear/side windows, street-level businesses,
floor heights, saved parcel profiles and original collisions remain unchanged.
The original random draws are retained. No runtime downloads or added binary
textures; no claim of photoreal acceptance. A later authored full-building
mesh should replace the opaque background wall itself. Actual gallery
capture after road and concrete integration; native Forward+ review required.
