Five individually authored designs with transparent backgrounds: faded sea-green TIDE lettering, a chalk moth symbol, weathered LAST SHIFT industrial stencil, overlapping torn service-paper notices, and a dusty rose freehand wave tag. SVG shapes and custom paths are original NightGrid work; no external fonts, ripped graffiti, third-party artwork, real phone numbers, or claims of actual gang identity. Five fixed wall-flush quads, shared unit mesh and cached material per texture; alpha lets the underlying brick/metal show, no emission, no lights, no collision, no shadows. Local anchors follow saved wall widths; within the low blank side walls, clear of conduits and dumpster. Material responds to normal scene lighting. Only the (-2,1) pilot. No changes to traffic, simulation, save format or RNG. Chunk replacement recreates the same named nodes and shared resources. Five transparent draws, not a performance measurement. Original artwork can be reused with new placement, but do not wallpaper every facade with the same design.
