# Brickwater wall storytelling — current integration

The five original SVG designs are already installed through `AlleySurfacePass/WallStorytelling` on main. Do not merge the outdated #154 runtime patch: it would introduce a second coplanar art layer. Current geometry uses a shared quad and cached per-texture material, with no new lights, shadows or colliders. The new regression checks single-layer placement, distinct textures, shared geometry, inward-facing quads and physical wall backing at centers and corners. Test results must come from the new branch preflight; native visual quality and frame timing are separate. Other Brickwater draft branches contain older geometry and shader variants and should not be wholesale merged over current photographic masonry, pavement and lighting work.
