# Chapter 1 — Your Future Has Been Optimized

2036. Morrow Bay. Synaxis Omnisystems, a fictional vertically integrated cloud, logistics and storage megacorporation, eliminates the protagonist's systems-operations job. The notice is deliberately sterile corporate language; the $75 severance is insulting. No real company or person is named.

QuotaDrop advertises self-employment while transferring expenses and risk to workers. Accept terms via the actual new-game menu; a stable severance receipt prevents repeat payment. Starter deliveries use inflation-adjusted $15–24 (2026 anchor) payouts. Existing delivery geometry is reused; no expensive new city system. New-game receipt selects the starter gig pay schedule after save restore; old sessions retain their existing courier pay schedule.

Mara's neighborhood referral pays $100 and qualifies the protagonist for a capsule tenancy. The $220 modern-price anchor yields a $296 initial deposit. With $75 severance and a $100 referral, six successful orders gross about $141, leaving a small buffer after the deposit. Expenses and optional purchases can extend the route. No car is required to finish on foot. The capsule is an enclosed berth in the existing physical room shell; a complete shared microhousing complex, recurring rent, actual food pickup storefronts and delivery animations are future work.

Longer-term arc: precarious work, making rent, saving for a cheap car, earning introductions to more lucrative contracts, progressing into independent work and influence. Satire should target incentives and institutional absurdity, with believable people, not caricatures. Don't trivialize poverty or imply the worker is to blame. Avoid mandatory crime or luxury purchases to advance.
