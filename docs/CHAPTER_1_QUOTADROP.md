# Chapter 1 — Your Future Has Been Optimized

2036. Morrow Bay. Synaxis Omnisystems, a fictional vertically integrated cloud, logistics and storage megacorporation, eliminates the protagonist's systems-operations job. The notice is deliberately sterile corporate language; the $75 severance is insulting. All characters and incidents are fictional.

## Executive Privilege

The six-stage interactive opening starts at a normal storage audit ticket, before the termination. The employee finds evidence of prototype data transfers by the CEO's stylish Shanghai-born executive assistant Lin Xue, who works with a fictional Chinese intelligence directorate. The same record reveals her affair with married CEO Jetson Wang. The player confronts executive management and chooses internal security or legal escalation; each produces its own corporate response, but both end in retaliation and the same pitiful severance. A separate exit interview offers feedback or refusal, with distinct corporate responses and no difference in payout. Her espionage is established through the fictional audit evidence, not her ethnicity or appearance. Neither path requires sabotage, combat, or illegal activity by the player.

The briefing includes a deadpan Mandarin call to a fictional PLA colonel, authored in simplified Chinese with English subtitles. Lin returns to English immediately and calls it customer support; Wang rationalizes the incriminating audit as an off-site. Security and Legal each have separate absurd bureaucratic retaliation lines. The humor targets executive hypocrisy, not language or nationality. The source Mandarin dialogue is retained for future voice recording; the current interface displays English subtitles to avoid relying on unverified CJK font coverage.

This first playable implementation uses the existing controller-accessible menu as a corporate terminal and dialogue sequence. A physical executive-floor office, authored character meshes, voice acting, cinematic blocking and environmental interaction remain future work.

QuotaDrop advertises self-employment while transferring expenses and risk to workers. Accept terms via the actual new-game menu; a stable severance receipt prevents repeat payment. Starter deliveries use inflation-adjusted $15–24 (2026 anchor) payouts. Six deterministic food orders resolve real restaurant and workplace addresses from existing catalogs. Each order requires explicit on-foot pickup/handoff; closed kitchens refuse new pickup until opening. Base pay plus stated tip varies by order, not arbitrary level scaling. No required purchase of customer's meal. Emergency recovery cancels a carried gig order without pay; a prepaid order is not charged to the courier. New-game receipt selects the starter gig pay schedule after save restore; old sessions retain their existing courier pay schedule.

Mara's neighborhood referral pays $100 and qualifies the protagonist for a capsule tenancy. The $220 modern-price anchor yields a $296 initial deposit. With $75 severance and a $100 referral, six successful orders gross about $141, leaving a small buffer after the deposit. Expenses and optional purchases can extend the route. No car is required to finish on foot. The capsule is an enclosed berth in the existing physical room shell; a complete shared microhousing complex, recurring rent, a reusable branded sealed takeaway bag appears on pickup, survives mid-order reload, hides in vehicles, and disappears at handoff or canceled order. The bag uses the existing weighted left-hand attachment; bespoke handoff poses and customer animations remain future work.

Longer-term arc: precarious work, making rent, saving for a cheap car, earning introductions to more lucrative contracts, progressing into independent work and influence. Satire should target incentives and institutional absurdity, with believable people, not caricatures. Don't trivialize poverty or imply the worker is to blame. Avoid mandatory crime or luxury purchases to advance.

## Satire continuity

The internal ticket has a zero budget and impossible deadline, compliance dashboards remain green under executive override, HR demands the employee train their AI replacement, and the gig platform uses the same institutional doublespeak. Six rotating journal notices are derived from completed deliveries without any new persistence, timers, or changes to compensation. Avoid slapstick accents and stereotyped characterization; the player should recognize how these systems absolve management and transfer risk to workers.
