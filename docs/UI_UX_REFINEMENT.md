# NightGrid interface refinement — November 21, 2026

## Goals
Reduce the monolithic text overlay, preserve visibility of the world, make priorities obvious, and ensure every menu is usable with keyboard, mouse and standard Xbox controls.

- Shared restrained graphite / teal / amber theme; contrast and a visible two-pixel focus stroke; larger buttons, left-aligned navigation and readable section headers. No external fonts or UI image assets.
- Title prioritizes Continue when a valid save exists. New-world confirmation defaults to keeping the existing session. Back restores the previous choice when available.
- Settings groups Audio, Controls, Display & Performance, with numeric slider readouts and focus-following scrolling. Existing settings.cfg keys, save/load semantics and controller input bindings remain unchanged.
- HUD: anchored player status and health meter, separate live courier objective and active contracts, circular radar at lower left, transient notices at top center, context-only action prompts at bottom. Driving and transit have distinct prompts; gamepad glyph names replace keyboard labels. Business dialogue has the same theme. Debug/performance data remains separate and F10 clean capture still hides HUD.
- City map keeps all real pins; a contrasted white player marker and north label improve orientation. Map updates at 10 Hz instead of every render frame.

## Interactive atlas follow-up
The paused city atlas accepts mouse click or arrow/D-pad cursor movement (8 m per step), Enter/A to pin, right-click/X to clear, Esc/B to return. The purple diamond and dashed straight-line bearing appear on both atlas and HUD minimap; HUD gives named district/nearby venue or service, distance and eight-way compass direction. Close clicks snap to the canonical walk-up service position. Destinations are clamped to the existing ±232 m city grid; no teleport, pathfinding claim, road alteration or objective replacement. Waypoints are optional two-number X/Z values in existing version-1 saves; older or malformed fields default to unpinned. New games begin without a pin. Test controller movement/placement/clearing, mission isolation, named venue snapping and save decoding. Native visual/physical controller test pending.

## Street navigation follow-up
Road routes use the authored fixed 9 × 9 intersections, bidirectional city centre-lines, and WorldPlan's 58 m block spacing. The planner projects start and destination to their nearest streets, evaluates nearby intersection endpoints and both Manhattan orders, and selects the shortest centre-line route (fewer turns break ties). It shares a cached result between atlas and HUD and recalculates as the actor moves at least one metre. Solid segments are road centre-lines, not lanes or traffic-aware recommendations; dotted last/first-mile links only indicate proximity, not a collision-free pedestrian path. Distance and turn guidance use world compass bearings, not camera orientation; interiors/transit show exit instructions instead of driving directions. No world, mission, player-control or save schema changes. Pure headless test covers known right/left and same-road routes, off-road access and 250 seeded bidirectional route pairs. Native driving and controller review pending.

## Circular local radar
The passive HUD now shows a 240 logical-pixel circular north-up local radar, anchored 24 px from the lower-left corner. The actor stays centred; 116 m radius (two city blocks) covers nearby streets, stations, venues, civic services, patrols and route geometry. All line segments are mathematically clipped to the circle including the search radius and route access legs; ordinary distant pins are omitted and mission/waypoint/contract targets use inward-contained edge arrows. Vehicle forward direction and on-foot visual heading drive the white centre arrow. Square interactive atlas remains unchanged, including keyboard/mouse/controller placement and clear destination; existing route planner remains the source. No extra viewport/render target, 10 Hz redraw. Dialogue hides/restores the radar; debug metrics move to upper-right. Validate the real HUD and clipping with hud_radar_smoke.gd, plus existing menu and civic navigation tests. Native HUD-inclusive views at 1080p, 720p and ultrawide still required. No screenshot or performance claim.

### Live road closures
Both circular radar and square atlas receive a copied live civic restriction catalog and draw an orange dashed edge with a transverse bar, using the canonical world intersection spacing. Radar clips the marks to its circle. A blocked/unavailable route keeps its purple destination pin or edge arrow but no longer suggests a direct dotted path; valid first/last-mile access links remain. Regression asserts the exposed edge coordinates and expiry, in addition to existing detour routing. No change to simulation, navigation decisions or saves.
