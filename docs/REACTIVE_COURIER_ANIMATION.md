# Reactive courier animation roadmap

Phase 1: fixed-tick direction-independent vector acceleration, explicit braking/reversal and air control, frame-rate-independent exponential facing, filtered gait speed, threshold hysteresis, smoothed playback rate and normalized phase-preserving loop crossfades. No gameplay top speed, stamina, save schema, physics frequency or collision shape change. Existing weighted rig/actions retained. The normalized phase is an approximation until motion clip foot-contact timing has been measured; it is not foot locking or motion matching.

Next phases: annotate actual foot contact and displacement, calibrate gait speed against physical velocity (retain gameplay responsiveness), add start/stop/pivot clips, implement post-animation slope/curb foot and pelvis adjustment with bounded probes, contextual hand IK, jump-height landings, fatigue, obstacle traversal and NPC LOD. Motion matching only after expanding and tagging motion data. Keep deterministic physics and native camera/controller/vehicle tests; no new permanent per-NPC processes.

Acceptance: actual rig playback, direction-independent acceleration, no instant reversal, bounded stop, stride continuity, no threshold thrashing, retained combat/vehicle/traversal/consumable regressions. Native 60 FPS and foot-sliding measurement remain outstanding. First-pass crossfades are not a full AnimationTree/motion-matching solution. Existing speeds deliberately retained pending native balance review.

References: Godot AnimationTree and AnimationPlayer documentation; Ubisoft GDC 2016 Motion Matching and the Road to Next-Gen Animation; Ubisoft predictive foot IK presentation. No proprietary Rockstar code or assets.

Phase 2 calibration tool: `godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/analyze_courier_gaits.gd` evaluates the actual imported skeleton at 64 samples per locomotion cycle, records minimum/maximum ankle height, stance candidates, contact phase and median horizontal stance speed per foot. CI prints JSON for review. These are estimates from in-place clips, not measured native foot sliding; no runtime sampling or speed retuning yet. Next use validated traces to align contact phase and calibrate playback before terrain IK.

Regression finding: integrated rail boarding failed after changing physical acceleration. Restore the exact RC6 component-wise 24 m/s² response (including air control and braking); keep exponential visual facing and gait crossfades. This intentionally supersedes the first-pass acceleration policy. Separate movement tuning from animation work. The offline gait analysis now writes JSON and uploads it as a standalone CI artifact early in preflight; avoids waiting for the full suite to retrieve the evidence.

Initial terrain correction: physics samples two rays under the actual animated ankles at most per eligible grounded slow-walk/idle tick. A custom SkeletonModifier3D runs after authored animation, solves each leg with a bounded vertical correction and preserves segment length; ankle orientation stays authored. Disabled while aiming, airborne, seated, during actions or sprinting; no capsule changes, no NPC processing. Phase heuristics are preliminary; native curb/slope checks, terrain-normal ankle alignment, pelvis lowering and true world-space foot locks remain outstanding. Godot 4.7 modifier documentation: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_skeletonmodifier3d.html.

Terrain follow-up: softly lower Hips toward the lower contact (maximum 12 cm), compensate each leg target for the pelvis shift, and rotate the authored ankle basis toward the sampled surface normal with a maximum 20-degree tilt weighted by approximate stance. Steep/invalid normals rejected; no additional probes. Test checks capped slope tilt, unmodified swing orientation, pelvis drop, limb length and state reset. This remains a preliminary slow-walk/idle solver, not yet a validated world-space foot lock. Visual evaluation on ramps and steps still required.

Bounded world-space foot lock: capture an ankle contact on static support at high stance weight, then compensate horizontal drift during the planted portion of the clip. Release on swing, missing/changed support, >14 cm displacement or >240 ms; cannot reacquire until swing. Dynamic and animatable supports excluded. Uses existing ray hits and solver, not new probes. This is a conservative slow-walk prototype; not full predictive foot locking, not measured slide reduction. Also correct the locomotion regression's braking duration to match the restored 24 m/s² policy (32 ticks rather than 30); the prior failure was an outdated test expectation.
