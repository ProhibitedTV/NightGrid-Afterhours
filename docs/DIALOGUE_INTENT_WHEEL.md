# Dialogue intent wheel — first playable slice

Original NightGrid screen-space design, inspired by intent-first conversation UX rather than copying another game's art or dialogue. Staff interviews use left/work, right/access, up/notes, down/leave. Binary mission decisions use a left/right pair, with a preview of the exact existing action before confirmation. The original action IDs, menu pause/cancel, notes, story choices, voice scene selection and caption-only fallbacks remain authoritative. No new recorded speech or runtime inference. Parking guard and street-contact lists remain unchanged in this slice; future authoring can group investigation topics and add canonical protagonist lines with offline voice IDs, meaningful consequences and native cinematic framing. Test keyboard, mouse and controller at 1080p and 720p; do not claim visual approval from headless checks.
