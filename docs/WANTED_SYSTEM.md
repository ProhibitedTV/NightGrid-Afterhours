# Witness-based wanted pilot
Five arcade stars, from clear to one through five response cruisers (plus two routine patrols). At zero heat the two regular patrol cars circulate on fixed existing roads; they are blue on the map, red markers are active response units. Their headlights/flashers are not enabled during routine patrol. More stars shorten dispatch intervals and increase response vehicle speed; no helicopters or tactical units yet.

Outdoor venue patrons visible during opening hours, ambient NPC drivers/bus drivers and functional patrol/response cruisers are real scene witnesses. An incident is tested against game-space range, facing and solid collision rays, never against a synthetic everywhere-present witness. Visible drawn weapon must be noticed continuously for 0.8 game seconds, then reports are rate-limited. Carrying holstered equipment is not a crime. Initial parked-car acquisition captures actual entry position and tests once at the next physics tick; subsequent entry of claimed cars doesn't levy a second report. No omniscient theft notification or automatic courier completion heat. Shots raise heat when observed or when hitting a police vehicle. All values are fictional game-balance units.

Police response cars receive a last reported position, refreshed only on officer sight while wanted or a new witnessed incident. No direct live player-node tracking after losing sight. After an eight-second quiet interval heat decays; HUD shows CLEAR / WEAPON NOTICED / IN SIGHT / SEARCHING / COOLING DOWN, stars and response count. Atlas/minimap mark routine and response cars and last-known search location. This is a bounded arcade pilot; no road-graph police pathfinding, large pedestrian crowds, realistic acoustic propagation, tactical police AI or real-world evasion simulation. Later passes should improve traffic-aware pursuit and authored pedestrian AI. Fixed 5 Hz perception with max 12 rays per query; incident-triggered queries only in physics, no render tick scene scans. Legacy scalar heat/claimed-car save format retained; optional validated last-known location added; pursuit units rebuilt rather than persisted. Native driving, HUD legibility and F8 performance validation required.
