# Seamless building interiors — initial city rollout

Goal: enter every actual building without loading screens, world swaps, or teleports. The ordinary lot implementation uses the existing staged sector streamer: stage zero builds a physical hollow ground-floor room at the real street address, and later stages furnish it while preserving the same collider. Players enter by walking; save positions remain ordinary world coordinates. Only nearby rooms receive a visible local light; inactive doors have no processing. Original street footprint, height and saved city manifest stay intact.

Scope: non-bespoke ordinary lots across all eight districts. Existing specialty West Docks, Central Exchange and Brickwater façades retain their existing collision/art contracts. Existing named venue and civic services still use their old interior system (H) until each is migrated. Landmarks and upper floors are not yet enterable. The first room layouts are modest reusable furnished spaces, not finished unique buildings. Next: migrate authored façades, real named business services, interior lighting proximity budget, multiple rooms and floors, and native traversal profiling. Keep every transition at real coordinates.

Test: tests/walk_in_lot_smoke.gd walks the real player into a stage-zero shell, checks the rear wall, finishes the streamed lot while occupied, saves/restores coordinates and walks out. Existing staged streaming and legacy interior suites remain enabled. Native visual/performance acceptance pending.

Second pass: real collidable inward-swinging doors and a proximity light controller (one visible unshadowed light). Door and light primitives are integrated into the streamed lot and world H input/HUD. Legacy named service anchors take precedence until migrated. Corrected initial GDScript Variant type inference failure. Ordinary door state is transient; no teleport on entry or exit.

Third pass: extend ordinary ground-floor depth to 12.6 m (within all authored lot depths), add a street lobby, central corridor and two real side rooms. Collidable dividing walls, lintels and front partitions are built in stage zero. Reusable fixtures remain outside the 1.9 m passages. Stable room identity is available from world coordinates for later service/audio/lighting integration. New smoke assertions walk the actual player into and out of a side room without relocation. No upper floors or bespoke façade migration yet.

Fourth pass: ordinary lots now include two accessible levels inside the original footprint (17 m internal depth). A continuous sloped access ramp in the west room leads to a second-floor landing and furnished upper level. Slab openings and guardrails are registered during the same collision-first streaming stage; no teleport or player movement rewrite. This is an initial ramp solution, not a finished staircase/elevator system. Added actual CharacterBody3D ascent and descent regression. Still pending: all remaining upper storeys, vertical lighting/camera refinement, named service conversion, bespoke buildings and native performance.

Fifth pass: single budgeted local light follows the active storey, exact upper-level world-coordinate restoration is covered by the traversal regression, and ordinary entrance thresholds reopen on restore to avoid closed-leaf overlap. Moved the focused interior regression immediately after project import, with timeout, script error rejection and explicit success marker, so failures are surfaced before the longer city suite. Native proof remains pending.

Compatibility fix: updated the existing photo-masonry regression to verify the new segmented physical shell rather than requiring the obsolete solid full-volume box. It checks each actual photographic exterior section against its collider, the exact original building envelope, and all existing texture-sharing/weather behavior. The earlier door revision failed the former solid-box assertion; the new test retains the material and collision coverage without insisting on an inaccessible building.

Performance pass: furnishings, worktops, and emissive fixtures are combined into lot-local MultiMesh batches grouped by material and level, with a 45 m detail visibility range. The exterior, structural interior surfaces, moving door, and all original physical collision shapes remain independent. Batches are freed with the parcel. Regression verifies actual instance accounting and visibility range; GPU performance on target hardware remains unmeasured.
