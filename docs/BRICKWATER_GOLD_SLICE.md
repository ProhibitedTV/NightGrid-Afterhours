# Brickwater gold vertical slice — first integration of acquired high-fidelity art

Target: the approved narrow, lived-in cyberpunk service alley; not a completed AAA scene. The first visual benchmark is the stable existing block (-2,1), centered at (-87,0,87). This branch combines the already pinned source packages (#153) with physical alley and original wall art (#143/#154). All upstream glTF, textures and SOURCE manifests are preserved without copy/re-download.

Real selected **source** mesh members, not kit showroom scenes: six window assemblies (large west/double east, upper street level), two industrial caged wall lights, two joined 2 m metal riser modules plus three support fittings, five electrical cable sections and six grouped waste/delivery props including hand truck, crates, carton and bags. Shared mesh cache; original materials, UVs and source asset names. Ground props sit on existing physical floor and have size-matched proxies. Two local shadowed warm lights only. Building shell and original wall paint remain, no fake traversable interior; existing upper facade silhouettes are not solved. No random placements, new save data or runtime downloads.
