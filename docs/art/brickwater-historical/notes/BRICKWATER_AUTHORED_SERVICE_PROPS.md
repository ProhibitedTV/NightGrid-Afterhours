# Brickwater authored service dressing

Stacked integration of #143 physical alley and #153 pinned CC0 sources. This pass uses six source packages (trashbag, cardboard_box_01, plastic_crate_01, hand_truck, industrial_caged_sconce, modular_pipes) at the existing fixed parcel (-2,1). Seven ground objects, two caged fixtures, and fourteen selected pipe pieces replace or complement the previous service geometry; no showroom layouts are instantiated into the world. Ground meshes use their measured local AABB for pavement contact and a co-rotated gameplay box collider. Mesh resources are cached and shared; nodes follow chunk lifetime. Two local lights are bounded to 4.6 m and do not cast extra shadows. These objects are static scenery, not a destruction-physics implementation.

No modifications to city roads, lot footprints, player saves, controls or transit. The apartment facade, fire escape and cable kits remain pinned but are not placed here: they need individual wall modules, verified facade geometry, supporting attachment points, and clearance review, rather than rendering the catalog layout. The pipe main and risers use straight source pieces and supports; junctions/end fittings and exact utility routing remain an art review task.

Engineering checks: offline nine-package audit before Godot import; expanded existing Brickwater smoke checks for mesh selection, imported PBR, ground collision, head clearance and centerline. Native acceptance remains pending: matched camera/clock Windows Forward+ images (dusk and daylight), physical mounting and traversal, source-scale review, wet material response, and measured frame timings on target hardware. No concept or headless test counts as native fidelity evidence.
