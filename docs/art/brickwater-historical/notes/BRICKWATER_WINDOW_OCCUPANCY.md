# Brickwater apartment occupancy lighting

The existing imported CC0 apartment window meshes have a distinct glass material primitive. Both permanent Brickwater parcels now choose stable, asymmetric dark/warm/cool occupancy states per bay and level; only the selected glass surface receives a shared copy of the original PBR material. Original frame, trim, geometry, alpha and material textures remain intact. The city day/night controller adjusts the two emissive palette entries, and emission fades to zero in daylight. No new lights, window nodes, collision changes, per-frame window callbacks, or actual interiors. The focused smoke test checks all 36 window states, source preservation, shared materials and night/day emission. This is a surface treatment and not native art acceptance; inspect visibility and alpha behavior in the new real engine captures.
