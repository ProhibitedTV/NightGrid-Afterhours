# Synaxis HQ: architectural precedent and dimensioned concept 01

Status: planning contract only; #460. Not a claim of implemented geometry, real-world code compliance, or native approval.

## Precedents (reviewed 2026-11-26)
- [C&P Corporate Headquarters, INNOCAD](https://www.archdaily.com/907562/c-and-p-corporate-headquarters-innocad): published ground and third-floor plans and architect description organize a compact seven-storey building around atrium/vertical circulation and building services. Apply a recognizable, vertically aligned core rather than floor pads.
- [Slack NYC HQ, Snøhetta](https://www.archdaily.com/803905/slack-nyc-hq-snohetta): published floor plan and description show distinct arrival/social/work/meeting zones. Apply a dedicated employee room, glazed collaboration spaces, and a continuous route from reception.
- [Zags HQ, Studio Razavi](https://www.archdaily.com/890310/zags-headquarters-studio-razavi-architecture): published plan and architect description illustrate partitioned corner offices within a flexible workplace. Apply glass fronts to meeting rooms while preserving privacy and wall-mounted signs.
- [Campbell Employee Center, KlingStubbins](https://www.archdaily.com/128264/campbells-employee-center-klingstubbins): architect description differentiates kitchen/servery/dining and front-of-house functions. Apply believable kitchenette, counters, sinks, waste stations, tables and storage; party occurs in breakroom, not an office aisle.
- [WBDG Interior Design for Research Facilities](https://legacy.wbdg.org/resources/interior-design-research-facilities): describes legible corridors and common areas; NASA research-building second-floor illustration shows central orientation and separate program spaces. Apply clear sightlines, room identifiers, and recognizable core.
- [UFC 4-610-01 (2021), appendix A pp. 83–86](https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/DOD/UFC/ufc_4_610_01_2021_c1.pdf): reviewed illustrated case studies with circulation percentages of usable area from 28–38%, modular workstation dimensions around 2.4 × 2.4 m, structural bays around 6–9 m. These are precedent benchmarks, not a mandatory commercial code.
- [US Access Board accessible routes](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/guides/chapter-4-accessible-routes/): accessible routes generally 915 mm continuous and 815 mm at short door openings. Our wider gameplay clearances are design choices, not a compliance certification.

No source floor plans or artwork copied into the repository. The committed SVG and JSON are original.

## Vertical program
B1: dedicated IT helpdesk near lift, separated server room with cold/hot aisle racks, electrical/mechanical spaces, secure storage, service corridor. Separate loud server ambience from occupied helpdesk. Ground: entry/visitor screening, reception, IP Security, records, meeting rooms, employee breakroom and enclosed toilets. L2–L5: perimeter daylight workstations in 2.4 m modules, meeting/focus rooms, copy/tea points, central circulation and core aligned with ground; department-specific labs only where appropriate. Level 14: private executive suite, assistant waiting area, conference room, HR interview room, executive lounge and terrace, same core alignment. Keep fictional floor numbering and existing chapter sequence; no fake traversable floors without geometry. Reserve intermediate floors as inaccessible until authored.
