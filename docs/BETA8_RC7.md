# NightGrid: Afterhours — Beta.8 RC7 | Executive Privilege

Windows x64 playtest candidate, Godot 4.7.2 / Forward+ / Vulkan. This candidate is published only from the exact current main revision after successful project preflight, Windows artifact export and native Windows launch smoke, and offline audio authoring verification. The downloadable payload is the already-tested executable/PCK pair, not a separate rebuild.

## What's new
- Chapter One: arrive at Synaxis headquarters, report to your cubicle, take the folder to basement IT, investigate the access alert and attend the executive briefing.
- Permanent headquarters in Glassline: 112 workfloor desks, robotics lab, executive suite and terrace, founder R9, employee parking and security dialogue.
- Staged lift transitions, staff conversations, employee circulation, printer/coffee interactions and story-aware office routines.
- Physical offboarding: return company equipment, collect personal belongings, leave through security, receive $75 severance once and start QuotaDrop.
- Offline dialogue production and SFX tooling for future reviewed assets. Missing audio retains existing fallbacks and/or subtitles.

## Installation
Extract the full Windows ZIP to a fresh folder. Keep NightGrid-Afterhours.exe and NightGrid-Afterhours.pck together. Read BUILD-INFO.txt and verify SHA256SUMS.txt. Back up previous saves; start a New World to experience Chapter One.

## Acceptance route
1. Start New World; watch or skip the arrival sequence. Walk through the cubicle area and collect the folder.
2. Take the lift to B1, hand off the folder and inspect the alert.
3. Explore departments, the robotics lab and parking guard if desired. Use printers/coffee; test interaction with keyboard and controller.
4. Ride the lift to level 14, attend the executive briefing, inspect the balcony, escalate the incident and complete HR.
5. Return to ground reception; accept the notice, return equipment, collect clothes at desk 04, walk to the street. Check the exact $75 credit and the first QuotaDrop order. Revisit HQ and check that access remains revoked.
6. Verify street driving, collisions, audio, streaming and save/load on a fresh profile. Capture F10 screenshots and F8 trace evidence with source SHA and graphics settings.

## Limitations
This is a release candidate, not a final game. The office character rigs, hand poses and staged elevator are provisional. Actual authored voice and additional generated SFX are not guaranteed to be present; the Anubis pipeline requires local generation, listening review and publication. Native visual quality, route clearance and 60 FPS on RTX 3060 are not established by automated headless/launch checks. Report bugs with logs and screenshots rather than treating the launch smoke as full gameplay certification.
