# Morrow Bay Police Department / central dispatch
The city has a fixed central headquarters at parcel (1,0), west lot, and a West Docks precinct at the existing Dock Watch Desk (-4,-1). Both are 24-hour public civic sites with original precinct room dressing, public counter and atlas shield, preserving existing Dock Watch contracts, collider and address. The headquarters is an enterable civic room accessed through the existing H/LB interior system (not a seamless new building mesh). It is prototype interior artwork, not a photorealistic authored architectural asset.

Each precinct is registered through civic site metadata (station_id, capacity, optional headquarters) with a stable address and curb staging location derived from its parcel. No hardcoded response jurisdiction grid: dispatch ranks available registered stations by street distance to the reported incident. To expand the city, extend the existing road network and add a station-tagged civic site in the new district. Runtime registration also rejects duplicate IDs and invalid coordinates/capacity. Central has 3 dispatch slots, West 2; total active + returning response fleet remains capped at 5 plus two existing routine cars. Units are identified and associated with their originating station, launch only at clear registered staging points, receive the existing reported location and drive through the police road controller. Released healthy cruisers drive back to their staging location before removal; disabled vehicles are retired. No separate garage vehicle mesh or off-map dispatch spawning. Case IDs and bounded 24-event dispatch history are session-local; saved legacy heat/last report rebuilds a case instead of restoring stale runtime unit nodes. Journal and wanted HUD include dispatch state; map shows police shields. Future phases: fully authored dedicated station architecture/garage, animated dispatchers and route-aware patrol reassignment, radio dialogue, saved non-player incidents and additional legal interactions. Native visual and performance verification required.
